# Privacy Policy — Link app

**Play and Track AB** · Effective 2026-09-13

This policy covers the **Link** mobile app for Android and iOS (package
`com.playandtrack.thelink`), published by Play and Track AB, Sweden.

## The short version

The app collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no
advertising and no cloud service. Everything it does happens between your phone
and your Link unit on your own home network.

## What the app does with data

**Stored on your phone only.** After setup the app remembers the local network
address and the name of your Link unit so it can open the remote directly next
time. This is kept in the app's private storage on the device and is removed
when you uninstall the app or set up a different unit.

**Sent to your Link unit only.**

- During first-time setup the Wi-Fi network name and password you type are
  sent over Bluetooth Low Energy directly to the Link unit, which stores them
  itself to join your network. They are not sent anywhere else and the app
  does not keep a copy.
- While you use the remote, the commands you tap (volume, input, mute and so
  on) travel over your local network to the Link unit. Nothing leaves your
  network.

**Never sent to us.** Play and Track AB operates no server the app talks to.
We receive no usage data, no device identifiers, no crash reports and no
personal information from the app.

## Permissions

- **Bluetooth** — to find a Link unit that is waiting to be set up and to pass
  it your Wi-Fi details. Used only on the setup screen.
- **Location (Android 11 and earlier only)** — those Android versions require
  the location permission for any Bluetooth scan. The app never reads,
  stores or transmits your position. On Android 12 and later, and on iOS, no
  location permission is requested.
- **Local network (iOS)** — to reach the Link unit on your Wi-Fi.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.
The Link unit itself may fetch signed firmware updates from a public download
server; that is a function of the unit, is described in its user manual, and
transmits no personal data.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with
